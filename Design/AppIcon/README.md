## 珊瑚红扇页提示词

Create one superbly designed macOS app icon for Finder Menu. This is an ORIGINAL BRAND SYMBOL, not a literal UI pictogram. Visual benchmark: the bold memorable silhouettes and restraint of Yuanbao and Apifox app icons. The previous concept was rejected because it was an ugly hamburger-menu plus cursor collage with cheap 3D gloss. Completely abandon that concept.

Direction: "fan of pages". A rich warm coral-vermilion rounded-square tile (#F45649), with ONE large pure-white graphic mark centered inside it. The mark is an abstract compact three-lobed fan: three broad rounded tabs sweep from lower left toward upper right, all joining into one harmonious curved base. Precisely carved generous coral negative-space channels distinguish the three lobes. It subtly evokes a menu unfolding, but should look like a beautiful confident standalone software brand, not a fan object, a WiFi symbol, a flower, an animal, three stacked horizontal lines, a cursor, a hand, or a letter.
Use masterful custom Bézier geometry: full rounded shoulders, flowing concave cuts, one restrained crisp termination, asymmetrical forward motion. Large simple forms, no thin lines or tiny details. The white silhouette must be a unified shape with a clean balanced visual center.
Style: mostly FLAT, vector-like graphic design. No folded-paper shading, no bevels, no extruded slabs, no plastic gloss, no glows, no texture. A very subtle tonal warmth across the coral tile is allowed but the white symbol stays perfectly flat white.
Square canvas 1024x1024. Native macOS superellipse tile at x=100..924 and y=100..924 approximately, continuous round corners. White symbol takes about 64 percent of tile width. Exactly centered front view, clean edge antialiasing. Real transparent alpha outside tile, no atmospheric halos or long shadows.
No text or letters, no presentation board, no mockup, no extra decorative objects, no badge, no sparkles. One icon only.

## 蓝紫双页提示词

Use case: logo-brand. Create ONE exceptionally refined macOS app icon for a Finder context menu utility. ORIGINAL distinctive identity with the bold rounded shapes and clean negative-space craftsmanship of Yuanbao and Apifox. No literal UI pictograms.

Concept: "two pages". Off-white rounded-square macOS icon tile. Center a bold BLUE-VIOLET abstract symbol suggesting two offset menu sheets interlocking. The symbol is made from TWO broad solid softly squared curved shapes, one rising at upper-left and one extending at lower-right. They tessellate to create one compact, visually balanced emblem. A generous uninterrupted white diagonal zigzag channel separates the shapes, like two gently stepped pages passing one another. The outer contour is mostly rounded and square-ish with a playful offset at upper-right and lower-left, NOT circular. One or two crisply cut inner corners provide contrast to the full rounded outer shoulders. No outlined pages. No drawn text lines. Do not use a mouse arrow, hamburger, hand, fan, flower, infinity symbol, literal letter, fox, or bird. No donut holes or chain links. Think beautifully hand-tuned abstract geometric software brand mark, instantly legible at 24 pixels.

Palette: white/off-white tile #FAFAFC, electric periwinkle-indigo symbol #6254E8, with only a tiny subtle shift toward #7B6CF0 across the two solid forms. The mark must be essentially FLAT and crisp. No 3D, gloss, bevel, folded paper, shadows on the symbol, streaks, sparkles, metallic texture, or translucent glass.
Composition: centered front-facing app icon, no perspective. 1024x1024 canvas. The rounded square tile is about 820x820 centered with continuous native macOS rounded corners. Symbol is about 530x530, generous balanced space. Real transparent alpha outside the tile; clean edges, no floating colored pixels, no colored halo, no external decorative marks. No typography. Exactly one icon, no board, labels or mockups.
Quality target: confident, youthful, restrained, precise. The two shapes should feel inevitable and harmonious, not like disconnected generic blocks.
